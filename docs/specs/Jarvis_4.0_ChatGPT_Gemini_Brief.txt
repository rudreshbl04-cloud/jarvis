1. WHAT JARVIS 3.0 IS

published. Jarvis 4.0 is the upgrade of the same app and repo, not a second
standalone project. Do not ask me to publish 3.0 first again.

the builder must check them before choosing model sizes or settings.

voice stopping after one command, actions only talking instead of doing,

The laptop is the server; the phone is the user interface. The phone is not
expected to run all the laptop AI models by itself.
